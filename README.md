#  VillAI 

**VillAI** is a video game created as part of a Final Degree Project (TFG) that explores the integration of artificial intelligence into gameplay, with a particular focus on enhancing the behavior and interactions of Non-Playable Characters (NPCs).

## 🎮 About the Project

In recent years, emerging technologies like artificial intelligence have profoundly transformed multiple industries by reshaping tasks that were once exclusively performed by humans. Areas such as information retrieval and learning have already seen significant change — and the video game industry is no exception.

One of the most evident impacts of AI in games is in the way we interact with NPCs. It’s common to find characters that repeat a single line of dialogue, regardless of context or timing, due to limited narrative relevance or development resources. This project addresses that limitation by asking: _Can AI enrich and expand NPC behavior and dialogue?_

**VillAI** answers that question with a resounding _yes_. It leverages generative AI models not only to create varied and context-aware dialogues but also to simulate more realistic NPC behavior — such as remembering past events, reacting dynamically to their environment, and speaking in a manner consistent with their personality.
